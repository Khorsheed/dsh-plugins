# Agent Note: Context Lens shows a tool call where it happened

Status: implemented

## Problem

A request's history in Context Lens lists tool calls and their results, but a reader often needs the scene around a call: what the conversation looked like when it ran. For example, eight identical `Replaced 1 occurrence in …` results came from eight different edits, and only the conversation shows which edit each one was. The lens could jump within its own panel, but it could not take the reader to the call in the conversation on the left. In a room, an external member's calls are not in the room conversation at all. They live in the member's own session.

## Decision

- **A call row offers 在会话中查看 when its calls are in this conversation**: the main agent's, and those of children it delegated by a tool call (they nest under that call). The jump finds the host's `data-chat-call-id` row (ui-tool ToolCallTree). A call not rendered yet pages history back to its turn through `loadThrough`, and a call the jump cannot find falls back to its turn. A conversation just switched to is still loading its own first page, and a `loadThrough` issued then pages nothing. This was seen on a disposable instance: `loadThrough(1148)` returned at once and turns 74–82 stayed the only ones rendered. So the jump (`locateCallPaging`) first waits for the conversation's first rows, then pages, and re-pages up to three times before it settles for the turn.
- **Folded step groups open one layer at a time.** The host keeps their rows in the DOM under `hidden="until-found"` and opens them on `beforematch` (ui-chat searchable-hidden). Opening is a React re-render, so the jump sends the scrollport one `beforematch`, then each frame re-finds the row and opens its outermost fold that is still closed. When the row renders, or after about 60 frames, it lands. This is the host's own reveal pattern (session-inspector `chat-node/reveal.ts`), without its `flushSync`.
- **A room member's call offers 在成员会话中查看.** It applies to a member run of a room round, keyed by the child session id. The room's lens panel cannot finish this jump itself: the right Sidebar is kept per session, so switching sessions unmounts the panel. The hand-over runs at the plugin level instead (`client/member-jump.ts`). It opens the member session with `uiWorkspace.openSession`, the entry the room's 轨迹→ uses. It waits until the right Sidebar's own on-screen session (`ctx.sidebarRight.mounted`, the one `openTab` acts on) is the member's and the lens kind accepts a tab there, giving up after 5 s. The session list can move first, and a tab opened in that gap would land in the room's Sidebar. Then it opens the lens tab with the `locateCall: { callId, hostTurn?, request?, session }` navigation. `request` is the request the reader was on (start time, request number). The member's lens selects the turn holding it and that request, so both lenses show the same context. Selecting the call's own turn showed an earlier context instead: round 79's #201 has 135 history messages with the edit result at 089 ×8, while the call's turn 71 has 84 messages with it at 071 and unique. The member's lens on #201 matches the room's, and both match what Kimi sent (wire request 74.1: messageCount 74 plus 63 kept user messages, 137, equal to `token_counting` length). The lens then pages to the call's own turn and lands on the call. `session` names the target, and a lens receiving a `locateCall` meant for another session ignores it.
- **The room's panel keeps its place.** Before handing over, it stores its selection (viewed session, turn, scope, request, main tab) in a module-level map keyed by its session. Every remount on the way back takes it, since a Sidebar may mount the panel more than once. It is dropped once the reader moves away from it, and it expires after 30 minutes. A newer navigation (a higher revision) wins over the stored place for good, unless that navigation is a call meant for another session. Re-clicking the header's lens button still opens the latest turn, as that button's contract says.
- **A located call opens and is outlined.** After landing, the jump clicks the call's own disclosure header when it is closed (ui-primitives DisclosureRow: `data-disclosure-row` with `aria-expanded`; nested calls' headers are skipped), exactly as the reader would, and outlines the call box with a self-removing Web Animations ring for about 2.4 s.
- **The reading travels with the call, and comes back with the place.** The hand-over also carries how the reader was reading that request: the composition filter and the open row, as its layer and position (the same request reconstructs the same rows in either session's lens). The member's lens applies them to that request only (matched by start time), once its detail is on screen, and opens that row into view. The kept place for the way back carries the same, plus the detail pane's scroll offset (used when no row is open). Verified on a disposable instance: room 85/85 · 尼尔盖曼 · #228 with 只看重复 on and 094 open → the member's lens on #228 with 只看重复 on and 094 open, the left pane on the turn-71 Edit call, expanded and outlined → the breadcrumb back to the room's lens with the same filter and 094 open.
- **An in-panel jump's way back restores the filter.** A jump to a row the active filter hides (看调用它的 under 只看重复) clears the filter. The jump trail records the filter in force at each jump, and 返回 puts it back.
- **Everything degrades silently.** Without `uiWorkspace.openSession` the member button is not offered. A throwing open stays put, and a session that never comes up ends the wait.

## Alternatives considered

- **Do the member jump inside the room's panel.** Rejected: the panel unmounts as soon as the session switches.
- **A host channel for "open session at anchor".** Rejected: not needed. The public open, the session binding's `loadThrough` and the DOM conventions were verified to be enough, on a disposable instance against the real room session.
- **Fire `beforematch` on every fold at once.** Rejected: an inner fold can be replaced by the outer one's re-render before it opens.

## Consequences

- If the member's call is older than the member lens's loaded turn window, the jump has no turn `startSeq` to page to. It then lands on the turn, or does nothing until that window is loaded.
- The browser's back button does not return to the room, because session switches add no history entry. The room's breadcrumb and the member session's 回到房间 do.
- Jumping by turn inside a member session was seen landing about 3,800 px off on the disposable instance. That is a separate issue: jumping by call id does not use the turn path.
